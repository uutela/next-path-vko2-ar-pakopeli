## Two corrections, before you read the rest

**1. This run measured the API quota, not the puzzles.** Fifteen of the twenty
requests never got a puzzle written at all: the key's free-tier quota ran out
during request 6 and every request after it failed with `429
RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`. Only five puzzle texts exist in this file. Nothing here
says how often the model writes a bad puzzle, because the model mostly did not
get to write one.

**2. The per-attempt verdict lines are unreliable; the `Result:` lines are
not.** The harness recorded writer calls and solver calls in two separate
lists and paired them by attempt number. When a writer attempt fails, no
solver call happens, and every later pairing in that request is off by one —
which is why attempt 2 of request 1 is labelled "solve-back — solver said
None" and then the request is accepted on that same attempt. The label is
wrong; the acceptance is right. `Result:` comes from the loop itself and can
be trusted. This is a defect in the measuring tool, recorded in `INBOX.md` and
left unfixed, because fixing it and re-running is a decision for the person
who asked for the measurement.

**What can honestly be concluded:** four puzzles passed every check and reached
memory; one request failed on timeouts and 503s; fifteen failed on quota. No
attempt was ever rejected by the schema check or the duplicate check. The
solve-back check reached a verdict at most five times.
